New Product Introduction (NPI) — Danish: Ny produktintroduktion
New Product Introduction (NPI) is the process by which a new product is launched onto the market or introduced into the company's supply chain. The purpose is to ensure a fast, seamless introduction where production, inventory, and logistics function optimally from the start.
What is NPI?
NPI covers the entire process from idea to launch:
Product Development –
Design and specifications for new products
Pilot production –
Testing of production, quality, and capacity
Inventory and Logistics
–
Planning of stock levels and distribution
Market Introduction –
Coordination of sales and marketing for an effective launch
Why is NPI important?
A structured NPI process helps to:
Reduce errors and delays during product launch
Ensure correct inventory and capacity from day one
Optimize collaboration between development, production, and logistics
Minimize the risk of overproduction or stockouts
