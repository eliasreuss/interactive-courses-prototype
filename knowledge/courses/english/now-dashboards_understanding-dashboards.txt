A Dashboard is a focused tool designed to present key performance indicators (KPIs) and important trends in a single, easily accessible location. Each Dashboard is created with a specific purpose, drawing directly from relevant Insights to ensure the information displayed is meaningful. Dashboards are constructed using two main interactive components: Widgets, which visually represent data, and Tables, which organize detailed information. This structure allows users to quickly monitor and analyze the most critical aspects of their operations.
