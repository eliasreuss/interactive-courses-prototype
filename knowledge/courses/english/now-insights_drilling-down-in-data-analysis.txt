Going one click deeper refers to the process of moving from broad data categories to detailed, item-level insights. For example, after filtering data using Double ABC Analysis, you can select a specific category such as AA to focus on your most critical items. By sorting these items by total cost of goods consumed, you identify the largest contributors. Clicking on a top item then reveals its individual performance metrics. This step-by-step approach enables users to achieve true transparency by examining data at the most granular level.
