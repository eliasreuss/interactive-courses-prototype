Widgets are components within Dashboards that provide quick, actionable insights. Each widget typically displays a key performance indicator (KPI) on the top left, showing the current value of a critical metric. The top right section shows your target for that KPI and indicates whether you are meeting it. To view detailed month-by-month data, you can hover over the trend line within the widget. This design helps users quickly assess performance and identify trends at a glance.
