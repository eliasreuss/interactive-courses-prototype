Inact Now provides a Safety Stock / Simulation insight that helps organizations establish and manage safety stock levels for different categories. This insight allows users to simulate various service levels and immediately see the impact on inventory costs. By adjusting service levels, users can evaluate the trade-off between higher product availability and increased inventory expenses, supporting informed decisions about optimal safety stock settings for each category.
