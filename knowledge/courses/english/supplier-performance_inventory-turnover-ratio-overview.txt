The inventory turnover ratio measures how often inventory is sold and replaced over a specific period. It reflects the effectiveness of stocking policies, lead times, and order volumes. A higher ratio indicates efficient inventory management, while a lower ratio may suggest overstocking or slow-moving goods. By strategically managing your supplier portfolio and using tools like Inact Now for comprehensive inventory visibility and root-cause analysis, you can improve your inventory turnover ratio without negatively impacting customer service.
