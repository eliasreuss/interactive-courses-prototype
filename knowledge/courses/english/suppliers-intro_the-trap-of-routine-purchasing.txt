The trap of routine purchasing refers to the tendency for procurement teams to focus only on immediate, day-to-day needs, such as placing orders to restock empty shelves and addressing urgent issues as they arise. While this approach ensures that basic business operations continue, it limits procurement to a reactive, support-oriented role. This mindset prevents the team from taking a strategic view of purchasing, missing opportunities for long-term improvement, cost savings, and value creation within the supply chain.
