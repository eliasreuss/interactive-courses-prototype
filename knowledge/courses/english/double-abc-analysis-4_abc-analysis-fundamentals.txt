ABC analysis is an inventory management technique that categorizes items based on their importance, typically using the Pareto principle or 80/20 rule. This principle suggests that a small proportion of items, known as A items, account for the majority of the total inventory value or sales. The remaining items, classified as B and C items, contribute much less to the overall value. By focusing on the vital few (A items) rather than the trivial many (B and C items), organizations can prioritize resources and management efforts for maximum impact.
