Effective inventory management involves applying different stock policies based on ABC categories. For A-items, which have high and stable demand, it is best to keep them consistently in stock to ensure high service levels for key products and customers. In contrast, C-items, which experience irregular and unpredictable demand, are better managed with buy-to-order or make-to-order policies. This approach helps avoid tying up cash in inventory that may not sell for extended periods, optimizing both service and financial efficiency.
