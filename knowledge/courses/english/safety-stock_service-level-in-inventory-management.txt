Service level in inventory management refers to the target set by a business to define the acceptable risk of running out of stock for a specific product. Unlike factors such as variance and lead time, which are often influenced by external market or supplier conditions, service level is a controllable, strategic decision. By setting a higher service level, a company aims to reduce the likelihood of stockouts, but this typically requires holding more safety stock. Conversely, a lower service level accepts a higher risk of stockouts but reduces inventory holding costs. The chosen service level directly impacts how much safety stock is maintained.
