Value chain thinking in procurement involves viewing procurement not just as a support function, but as a strategic driver that contributes directly to a company's competitiveness, innovation, and sustainability. This approach requires a mindset shift, recognizing that procurement decisions can influence the overall value delivered to customers and the business. By adopting value chain thinking, organizations position procurement as a key player in achieving business goals and maintaining a competitive edge.
