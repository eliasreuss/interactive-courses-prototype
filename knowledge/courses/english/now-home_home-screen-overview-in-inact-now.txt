The Home screen in Inact Now is organized into three main sections: Dashboards, Actions, and Insights. These sections provide users with a personalized overview based on their specific role and responsibilities within the organization. Depending on your assigned tasks and the information shared with you, you may see all three sections, a combination of them, or possibly none. This tailored approach ensures that each user has quick access to the most relevant tools and information for their daily activities.
