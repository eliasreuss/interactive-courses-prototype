Managing reorder points for a single product can be done manually, but handling this process for many products is complex and time-consuming. Automation is essential for efficiency and accuracy. Inact Now automates reorder point management by continuously tracking sales patterns, lead times, and safety stock levels. This ensures that reorder points are updated daily, helping businesses maintain optimal inventory levels and reduce the risk of stockouts or overstocking.
