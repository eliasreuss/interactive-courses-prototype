Conducting a structured spend analysis is essential for uncovering inefficiencies in procurement and expenditure. By first establishing an overview and mapping your data to answer key questions about your organization's spending, you can reveal issues and opportunities that may have previously gone unnoticed. This process helps organizations gain clarity, identify cost-saving opportunities, and make more informed decisions regarding their supply chain and purchasing strategies.
