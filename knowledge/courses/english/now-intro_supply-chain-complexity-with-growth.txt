As a business expands, its supply chain becomes more complex due to the need to serve more customers, manage a broader range of products, and coordinate with an increasing number of suppliers. This added complexity can overwhelm operational teams, making it difficult to maintain a clear overview of operations. As a result, teams may resort to reactive problem-solving and rely on intuition rather than data-driven decisions, which can negatively impact efficiency and performance.
