Supplier performance is crucial because suppliers are not just providers of goods or services, but key partners that directly influence a company's financial results, innovation capabilities, and competitive position. A significant portion of a company's spending is often allocated to external suppliers, so effective management of these relationships can have a major impact on profitability, operational efficiency, and the ability to achieve long-term strategic goals. Ensuring strong supplier performance helps organizations remain competitive and supports sustainable growth.
