End2End has two different ways of narrowing data, and the difference matters because they act at different points in the calculation.

Filters apply before the data sources are linked. They reduce the amount of data that goes into building the relations at all, so a record removed by a filter never becomes part of any supplier-product or product-customer relation. The Edit Filter setup window states this directly: filters "help reduce the amount of data and ensure that only relevant information is used when creating relations between Data sources".

Slicers apply after the linking, on the data that is already related. They narrow what you see without changing which relations exist underneath.

The practical rule: use a filter when the excluded records should not count as connections at all — for example excluding inactive suppliers or discontinued items so they do not inflate the relation counts. Use a slicer when you want to look at a subset of a chain that is otherwise correctly built — for example viewing only Danish customers within the existing relations.

Both are shown in the info dialogs: the info icon next to a column heading lists that data source's active Slicers and Filters, and the info icon next to the view name lists them for the whole view.
