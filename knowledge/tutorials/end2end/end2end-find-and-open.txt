Click End2End in the top navigation to open the library, then click the name of a view in the End2End views column to open it.

If you have many views, use the Search box at the top right of the library. Type part of the name and the lists filter as you type; the "Showing N of N" counter next to the box tells you how many items match out of the total. The search covers both End2End views and Heatmaps, so a match may appear in either column.

If you cannot see the view you expect, check whether its group is collapsed — click the arrow next to the group name to expand it. Also remember that you only see views for the data sources you have access to, so a colleague may see views that you do not.
