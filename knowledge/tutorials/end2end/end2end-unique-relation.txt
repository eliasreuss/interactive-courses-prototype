When you have selected one or more buckets, a Unique button appears in the link area between the columns, and a "Unique Relation: OFF" indicator appears in the page header.

By default (OFF), a product is included if any of the selected suppliers deliver it — even if other, unselected suppliers deliver it too. With Unique switched ON, only products that come exclusively from the selected suppliers are counted. Products with a second source are excluded.

You switch it on by clicking the Unique button. The header sentence then changes to include the word "exclusively", the summary card of the affected column is marked with a green Unique tag, and the numbers update. Click the button again to switch it back off.

Unique is the setting to use for dependency and risk questions — "which products would we lose if this supplier group stopped delivering?" — because it separates genuine single-source exposure from products where an alternative supplier already exists.
