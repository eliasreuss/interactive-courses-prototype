Next to the aggregation dropdown in the settings panel are two small toggle buttons that change how the numbers in that column are displayed. They only affect the display — the underlying calculation is unchanged.

0,0 — decimals. When active, values are shown with decimals (25,99). Click it to switch decimals off and show rounded whole numbers (26). This is useful when you want a cleaner column, or when the decimals are noise, for example with Count.

% — percentage. When active, each bucket tile shows its value as a percentage of the column total instead of as an absolute number. With a Count aggregation the bucket percentages add up to 100 %, which makes it easy to see how the population is distributed — for example that C-suppliers are 57 % of all suppliers. With Avg the percentages are relative to the column's overall average, so a bucket that scores twice the average shows around 200 %.

Each of the three columns has its own pair of buttons, so you can show suppliers as percentages while keeping products as absolute values. Click Save to store the choice.
