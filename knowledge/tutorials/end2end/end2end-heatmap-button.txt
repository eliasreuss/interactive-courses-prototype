Each link area contains a Heatmap button. It is the entry point to the heatmaps built on that particular relation — supplier-to-product for the left link, product-to-customer for the right link.

Click the button and a dropdown opens containing New Heatmap at the top, followed by a searchable list of the heatmaps that already exist for that relation. Click an existing name to open it, or New Heatmap to build one from the current relation.

There is also a green Create Heatmap button at the top right of the End2End view, which creates a heatmap from the view as a whole.
