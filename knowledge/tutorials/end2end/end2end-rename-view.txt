---
topic: How to rename an End2End view in Inact Now
source_type: tutorial
language: english
keywords: rename, change name, edit title, pencil icon, view name
product_area: End2End
question_type: how-to
---

Open the End2End view and click the small pencil icon just to the right of the view name at the top of the page. A Change name window opens with a Name field containing the current name.

Type the new name and click Save, or click Cancel to leave it unchanged. The new name applies immediately and is what colleagues will see in the End2End library.

Because views are shared, pick a name that describes the analysis rather than the person — "Single-source risk, EU suppliers" is more useful to the next colleague than "Elias test".
