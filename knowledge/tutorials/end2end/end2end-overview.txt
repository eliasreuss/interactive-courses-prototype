End2End is the module in Inact Now where you follow the value chain across three linked data sources at once: Suppliers, Products and Customers. It answers questions that a single Insight cannot, because it connects data that lives in separate tables — for example "which customers buy the products that come from my C-suppliers?" or "how much of my revenue depends on products from a single supplier?".

You open it from End2End in the top navigation bar. The landing page is a library with two sections: End2End views (the three-column analyses described in these guides) and Heatmaps (a separate visualisation built on top of a relation).

An End2End view always shows the same layout: a Suppliers column on the left, a Products column in the middle, a Customers column on the right, and a link area between each pair of columns showing how many relations connect them. Each column is split into the categorisation buckets used in your data — A, B, C and Inactive for suppliers, the Double ABC matrix (AA, AB, AC, BA, BB, ...) plus lifecycle groups for products, and Protect, Develop, Grow and Turnaround for customers.

The core way you work in an End2End view is by clicking buckets. Clicking a bucket narrows the whole chain to just that part of the business, and the other two columns recalculate to show only what is connected to your selection.
