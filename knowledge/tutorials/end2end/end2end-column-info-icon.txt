Each of the three column headings — Suppliers, Products and Customers — has a small info icon beside it. Hover over it to see a tooltip describing the state of that data source:

The Slicers currently applied to it, or "None".
The Filters currently applied to it, or "None".

When you have selected buckets, the tooltip also opens with a sentence describing the current selection, for example "A + B suppliers purchase 1744 products", and your selection appears in the slicer list expressed as a condition such as "Supplier categorization = A, B".

Use it as a quick check before you trust a number: if a column is narrower than you expected, the tooltip tells you exactly what is narrowing it.
