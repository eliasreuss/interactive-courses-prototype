In the Grid view of an Insight, you can customise which columns are visible and in what order.

1. Click the column layout icon (the grid/table icon on the far right of the small toolbar above the data table — to the right of the download button).
2. The Column layout panel opens, showing two sections:
   - Shown columns — the columns currently visible in the table.
   - Hidden columns — the columns available but not currently shown.
3. Click the eye icon on any column to toggle it between shown and hidden.
4. Drag any column up or down using the handle on its left to reorder it.
5. Use the Search columns box at the top to quickly find a specific column by name.
6. Click Save to apply your changes, or Close to discard them.

Your saved column layout persists the next time you open this Insight.
