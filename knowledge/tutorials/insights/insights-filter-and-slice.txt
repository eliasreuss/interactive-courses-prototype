The filter bar runs across the top of every Insight, just below the title. It contains dropdown filters — for example, Item Group, Product Group, Item Status, Responsible. Selecting a value in any dropdown instantly updates the data shown to match that selection.

To reset a filter, click its dropdown and select the blank option at the top of the list.

The green + button on the left of the filter bar lets you add a temporary personal filter. Click it to open a searchable list of available columns, then click one to add it as a filter for your current session.

You can also search by item ID using the Search Item ID box on the right side of the filter bar. This is useful when you want to look up a specific product, supplier, or customer directly.

The Showing N of N counter near the search box updates as you filter, telling you how many rows match your current selections out of the total available.
