Click Insights in the top menu bar to open the Insights library.

Insights are organised into groups (like Inventory, Suppliers, Products, Customers) and subgroups within each (like Stock Policy, ERP Planning Parameters). Each group shows a number indicating how many Insights are inside it.

To see the Insights inside a subgroup, click the ‹ arrow on the right side of the subgroup row. The list expands to show each Insight with its name, owner, and data source.

To open an Insight, just click its name.

If you're looking for something specific, use the Search box near the top-right of the library to filter by name as you type. You can also use the Owner, Data source, and Group dropdowns to narrow the list further.

Once you're inside an Insight, you can jump quickly between your recently opened Insights by hovering over Insights in the top menu — they appear in a small dropdown.
