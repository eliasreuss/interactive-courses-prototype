topic: How to check when an Insight's data was last updated in Inact Now
source_type: tutorial

product_area: Insights

---

Every Insight shows an orange "Updated X ago" badge in the toolbar (e.g. "Updated 2 months ago"). Clicking it opens a "Data is not up to date" dialog showing the exact date and time of the last data update, and how long ago that was.

If the data is outdated, the dialog suggests contacting the person responsible for the Insight or reaching out to Inact support at support@inact.io.
