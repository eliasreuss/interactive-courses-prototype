1. Click Insights in the top menu bar to open the library.
2. Click the green + New Insight button in the top-right corner.
3. A Choose template window opens. It has tabs for each area — Inventory, Suppliers, Products, Customers, ERP Data Validation — plus a Create without template button.
4. Choose how to start:
   - Use a template: Click a tab to browse ready-made Insights grouped by category (e.g., under Inventory: Stock Policy with Double ABC Analysis, New Items, Discontinued Items, Dead Items, Strategic Items; or ERP Planning Parameters with Safety Stock, Safety Stock | Simulation, etc.). Tick the checkbox next to any template(s) you want.
   - Start from scratch: Click Create without template to begin with a blank setup.
5. The Setup page opens. Fill in:
   - Data source — which type of data this Insight is built on (Products, Customers, or Suppliers).
   - Owner — who owns this Insight (defaults to you).
   - Status — Public (visible to everyone) or Private (only visible to you).
   - Included columns — select which metrics/columns to include by moving them from the Available columns list to the Included columns list.
6. Once you've added at least one column, click Continue to finish creating the Insight.
