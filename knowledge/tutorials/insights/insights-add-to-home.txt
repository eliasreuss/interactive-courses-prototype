You can pin an Insight to your Home page so you can access it quickly without searching through the library.

While viewing any Insight, click the Add button (the house/home icon) next to the Insight title. The button will change to "Added to Home" and a confirmation message appears at the bottom of the screen.

The Insight will now appear in the Insights section at the bottom of your Home page.

To remove it from the Home page, go to the Home page, click Edit next to the Insights heading, and remove it from the Selected Insights list.
