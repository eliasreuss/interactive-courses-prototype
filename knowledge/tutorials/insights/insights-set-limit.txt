The "Set limit for this Insight" field, found in the Setup panel, controls whether the Insight shows only the top or bottom N rows, ranked by a specific column.

Clicking the "Set limit" button expands three controls:
Select column dropdown — pick the column to rank rows by. The list shows all columns currently in the Included list.
Top / Bottom dropdown — choose "Top" to show the highest values, or "Bottom" to show the lowest.
Number of rows dropdown — choose how many rows to show: "All rows", 5, 10, 25, 50, 75, or 100.

After configuring, click the green checkmark to confirm, or the red X to cancel without changes.
