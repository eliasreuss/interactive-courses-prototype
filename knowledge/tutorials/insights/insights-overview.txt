Insights are the core analytical tool in Inact Now. Each Insight is a focused view of your data — a list of items (products, suppliers, customers) with the metrics that matter for a specific business question.

You access all your Insights by clicking Insights in the top menu bar. This opens the Insights library — a list of every Insight you have access to, organised into groups like Inventory, Suppliers, Products, and Customers.

Opening an Insight shows you a data table where each row is a product, customer, or supplier, and each column is a metric (e.g., Revenue 12 mos., Net Profit, Inventory Value). You can filter, sort, and slice this data to zero in on exactly what you need.

Depending on how the Insight is configured, it may offer different views: Grid (a flat data table), Matrix (a visual categorisation layout), or KPI (summary metric tiles). Not all Insights have all three — it depends on what the Insight is designed to show.
