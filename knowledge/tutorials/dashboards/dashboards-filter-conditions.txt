When you click + Dashboard filter and select a column, the Data slicer dialog opens with a condition dropdown. The available conditions depend on the column type:

For numeric columns: Greater, Equal or greater, Less, Equal or less, Equal, Not equal, Between, Not between.

For text/categorical columns: Equal, Not equal, Contains, Not contains, Starts with, Ends with, Not starts with, Not ends with.

After choosing a condition and entering a value (or selecting values), the dialog offers three buttons:

Save — saves the filter without refreshing data.
Save & Refresh — saves the filter and immediately refreshes all data in the dashboard.
Cancel — discards the filter.
