Each KPI card on a dashboard tells a story about a single metric. Here's what the different parts mean:

The large number is the current value of the metric — for example, 89,61 for OTD Outbound %.

The target appears in the bottom-right or top-right, shown as "min. 98,00" or "max. 2.500.000" with a small clock icon. A minimum target means the value should be at least that number. A maximum target means it should not exceed that number.

The change label (e.g., "▲ 2,91% Since last month" or "▼ -32,54% Since start 2026") shows how the value has moved compared to a reference point. Green and red colouring depends on whether the metric is set as Positive or Negative trend.

The performance label at the bottom of the card (e.g., "8% below" or "252% above") tells you directly how far off the target you are.

The trend chart on large cards shows historical performance over recent quarters, with a dashed line marking the target.

The card background turns pink or red when a metric is below its minimum target — a quick visual signal that something needs attention.

Clicking any KPI card takes you directly to the underlying Insight for more detail.
