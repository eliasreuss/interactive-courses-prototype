KPI cards are the metric tiles at the top of a dashboard. Each one shows a single number pulled from your data.

1. Open the dashboard and click Edit in the top-right.
2. Click the Dashboard tab.
3. You'll see the current KPI cards laid out in rows. Empty slots show "No KPI — Click to add KPI".
4. Click an empty slot (or click Click to edit on an existing card) to open the Edit widget window.
5. In the window, you'll see a table of your data. Click the cell that contains the value you want this card to display. The Label field at the top will update automatically with the metric name — you can edit this to change what's shown on the card.
6. Set the Trend to control how the colour coding works:
   - Positive — green means the number went up, red means it went down (good for metrics like revenue or delivery performance).
   - Negative — green means it went down, red means it went up (good for metrics like excess inventory).
   - Neutral — no colour coding.
7. Click Save.

To add a new row of cards, click + Add KPI row at the bottom of the layout. To remove a card, click the bin icon in the top-left corner of that card.
