The Home page in Inact Now can show KPI cards and data from one or more dashboards. You choose which dashboards appear there.

1. Open any dashboard.
2. Click Edit Home in the top-right corner (the house icon).
3. A window opens showing two columns:
   - Left side — dashboards not currently on your Home page, listed by group. Each has an Add → button.
   - Right side — dashboards already on your Home page. The one you're currently viewing is marked "Currently viewing".
4. Click Add → to move a dashboard to the Home page, or click × on the right side to remove one.
5. Click Save.

Any dashboard included on the Home page shows a small house icon next to its name in the Dashboards list.
