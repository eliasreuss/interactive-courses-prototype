Dashboards are organised into named groups on the Dashboards list page — for example, all inventory-related dashboards might sit under an "Inventory" group. This keeps things tidy when you have multiple dashboards.

To move a dashboard to a different group:
1. On the Dashboards list, click the ••• button next to the dashboard.
2. Select Change group.
3. In the window that appears, select or type the name of the group you want to move it to.
4. Click Save.

To rename a group:
A group can be renamed by clicking the appropriate option when managing it. A window appears with a Name field — update it and click Save.

To delete a group:
Deleting a group is permanent and cannot be undone. A confirmation window will ask you to confirm before anything is removed.

Dashboards that haven't been assigned to a group appear under an "Unsorted" section.
