In the Settings tab of dashboard edit mode, the Connect End2End Setup dropdown lets you link the dashboard to an End2End configuration, allowing End2End data to flow into the dashboard.

To use it, open the dashboard, click Edit, go to the Settings tab, and select the relevant setup from the Connect End2End Setup dropdown. Click Save when done.
