You can have any dashboard delivered to your email automatically on a schedule.

1. Open the dashboard you want to receive by email.
2. Click the Subscribe button (the envelope icon) in the top-right corner.
3. Choose how often you want to receive it from the Select schedule dropdown. Options include:
   - Daily on weekdays at 8:00 or 12:00
   - Daily every day (including weekends) at 12:00 or 23:00
   - Weekly on a specific day of your choice
   - Monthly on the first or last day of the month
4. The Include table toggle is on by default — this means the email will include both the KPI cards and the data table. Turn it off if you only want the KPI summary.
5. Click Subscribe.

You'll receive the dashboard as a snapshot on the schedule you chose. To unsubscribe, click Subscribe again on the same dashboard — the option to cancel your subscription will be shown.
