Deleting a dashboard is permanent — it cannot be recovered afterwards.

1. Click Dashboards in the top menu bar.
2. Find the dashboard you want to delete and click the ••• button on the right side of its row.
3. Select Delete (shown in red).
4. A confirmation message appears asking if you're sure. Click Delete to confirm, or Cancel to go back.

Once deleted, the dashboard is gone. Any email subscriptions to it are also removed.
