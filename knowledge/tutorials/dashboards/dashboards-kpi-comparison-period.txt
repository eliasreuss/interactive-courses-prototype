KPI widgets with a trend arrow compare the current value against a previous period. To change the comparison period, click the small dropdown arrow on the KPI widget. The available options are:

- Since last month
- Since 3 mos. ago
- Since last year
- Since start 2026

Select the period you want and the widget updates to compare against that timeframe.
