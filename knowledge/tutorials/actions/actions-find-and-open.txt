To find and open an Action list, click Actions in the top navigation bar to go to the Actions library.

The library shows all lists grouped by category. If a group is collapsed, click the arrow on the right side of the group row to expand it and see the lists inside.

To search by name, type in the Search bar at the top right. The list updates as you type.

To narrow down by owner, creator, data source, or group, use the filter dropdowns along the top: Assigned to, Created by, Datasource, and Group. Select a value from any dropdown and the library will show only matching lists.

Once you find the list you need, click its name to open it.
