Assigning an Action list gives ownership to a specific team member so it is clear who is responsible for working through it.

Open the Action list and click the gear icon in the top right corner. Choose Assign from the dropdown. The Assign Action window opens.

In the Assignees field, the currently assigned person is shown. Click the field to search for and select a team member. You can assign to multiple people. To remove someone, click the × next to their name.

Optionally, set a Deadline by picking a date, and choose whether to automatically subscribe the assignees to email updates by ticking Subscribe all assignees to this Action and selecting a notification plan.

Click Save to confirm. The assigned person's name will appear in the Assigned to column on the Actions library page.
