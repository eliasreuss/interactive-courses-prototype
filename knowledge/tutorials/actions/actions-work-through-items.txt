Open an Action list by clicking its name in the Actions library. Inside, items are grouped by their current status — you will see sections like TO DO and DONE, each with a count badge.

Click the arrow next to a status section header to expand it and see the items inside. Each row shows the item along with data columns relevant to that list.

To change an item's status, click the checkbox next to it to select it. Once selected, the toolbar at the top updates to show available actions — you can delete the selected item or use the status buttons at the top of the page (TO DO, DONE, or any custom statuses) to move it.

You can also click directly on a row to open the item detail panel, where you can review the item's data and then use the status buttons to move it.

To select all items at once, tick the checkbox in the column header row.
