You can take action on several Action lists at once from the Actions library page.

Tick the checkbox on the left side of each Action list row you want to include. As you select items, a bar appears at the bottom of the page showing how many lists are selected and what you can do with them.

The available bulk actions are:
- Assign — assign all selected lists to a team member at once
- Change layout — apply the same status layout to all selected lists
- Change group — move all selected lists to a different group
- Change author — change the author/creator on all selected lists
- Delete — delete all selected lists at once (you will be asked to confirm)

To cancel your selection, click Deselect in the bottom bar.
