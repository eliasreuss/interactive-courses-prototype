Action lists come with TO DO and DONE statuses by default. You can add extra status categories to represent intermediate steps in your workflow.

Open the Action list. In the status bar at the top, click the + button to the right of the existing status buttons. A new entry appears with a colour swatch and a text field that says Category name.

Click the colour swatch to pick a colour for the new status. Then click the Category name field and type the name you want — for example, "In Review" or "Escalated". Click the green checkmark to confirm, or the red X to cancel.

The new status button will appear in the status bar and items can be moved into it the same way as TO DO or DONE.
