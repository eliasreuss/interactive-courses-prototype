For End2End Relation Actions, the Edit Action list dialog includes an End2End settings section with three filter areas: Suppliers, Products, and Customers. Each area has a "+" button to add a slicer filter that restricts which items from that data source are included.

The Products area also has a Unique toggle button, which controls whether products are counted once each or allowed to repeat across matched relations.
