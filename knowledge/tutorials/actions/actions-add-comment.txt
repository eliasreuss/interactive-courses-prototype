You can leave comments on an Action list to communicate with your team or leave notes about the work.

Open the Action list, then click the comment bubble icon in the toolbar on the right side of the page. A Comments panel opens on the right, showing any existing comments and a search bar.

Type your comment in the input area and submit it. Comments are visible to anyone who opens the same Action list.

To search through existing comments, type in the search bar at the top of the panel. Click the comment icon again to close the panel.
