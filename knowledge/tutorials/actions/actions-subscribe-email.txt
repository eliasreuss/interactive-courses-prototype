You can receive email updates about an Action list so you stay informed without having to check it manually.

Open the Action list, then click the envelope icon in the toolbar on the right side of the page. The Subscribe to Action window opens.

There are two subscription options. Tick Action Subscription to receive a regular status email. Use the dropdown below it to choose how often you receive it — for example, "Daily (Monday-Friday) at 8:00". Tick Alert mail to receive a notification each time a new item is added to the list.

You can enable one or both options. Click Subscribe to save. To stop receiving emails, return to the same window, untick the options, and save again.
