Every Action list in the library shows a status progress bar in the Status column. This bar gives you an instant view of how much work has been done.

The bar fills from left to right as items are moved to a completed status. To the right of the bar you see the count in the format X of Y — for example, "6 of 6" means all items are done, while "0 of 50" means no items have been actioned yet. Above the bar, a percentage (such as "0%") shows the overall completion rate.

When all items are done, the bar turns fully green and shows the label Done.

Each list also shows a Last activity date, which tells you when an item was last moved or updated. This helps you quickly spot lists that have been neglected or are actively being worked on.
