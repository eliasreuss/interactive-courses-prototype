---
topic: How to change the author of a single Action list in Inact Now
source_type: tutorial
language: english
keywords: change author, creator, reassign author, action list, dropdown menu
product_area: Actions
question_type: how-to
---

To reassign who is recorded as the creator of a single Action list, open the Actions library, click the "..." (or ▾) dropdown on the right side of the Action list's row, and choose Change author. Select the new author from the list of users and confirm.

To change the author on several Action lists at once instead, select them with the checkboxes and use the Change author option in the bulk action bar.
