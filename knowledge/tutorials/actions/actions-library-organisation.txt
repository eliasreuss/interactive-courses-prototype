The Actions library page shows all Action lists organised in a two-level group structure. At the top level you see master groups such as Inventory or Products — these represent broad categories that match your data domains. Each master group expands to show subgroups like Category Management or Unsorted, and the Action lists sit inside those subgroups.

Each group row shows a number badge (how many Action lists are inside) and a name. Click the arrow on the right to collapse or expand any group. The Collapse all and Open all links at the top let you manage all groups at once.

Each Action list row shows: the list name with an info icon, a status progress bar showing percentage complete and item count, the date of last activity, who the list is assigned to, and who created it. A recurring arrows icon indicates the list updates automatically when the underlying data refreshes.

To find a specific list, use the Search bar at the top right. You can also filter the view by Assigned to, Created by, Datasource, or Group using the filter dropdowns.
