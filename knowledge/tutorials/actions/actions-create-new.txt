From the Actions library page, click the + New Action button at the top right. A window titled Choose Action template opens.

The templates are organised across two tabs: Inventory and Products. Each tab contains template groups — for example, Inventory has Stock Policy, ERP Planning Parameters, and Working Capital. Click the arrow on a group to expand it and see the individual templates inside, such as Update Responsible, Update Item Group, or Too Slow to Stock.

Click the checkbox next to a template to select it. You can select multiple templates at once using Select all to add them all in one step, or expand a group and pick individual ones. When ready, confirm your selection to create the Action list.

If none of the templates fit your needs, you can create a custom Action list directly from an Insight — see the guide on creating an Action list from an Insight.
