Click Show in Grid on the right of the heatmap toolbar to open the records behind the matrix as a list.

Because a heatmap normally crosses two data sources, a small window appears first — "Choose a grid to open" — offering one entry per data source with the number of records in each, for example "214 Suppliers" and "2.006 Products". Click Open grid next to the one you want.

The grid that opens is the standard Inact grid: a searchable, sortable table with a Showing N of N counter, column controls, and buttons for downloading and copying the data. Each row has an icon on the left that opens that record's detail panel.

If you have drilled into the heatmap first, the grid contains only the records inside your drill-down, which makes this the natural way to go from "this cell looks wrong" to the actual list of items behind it.
