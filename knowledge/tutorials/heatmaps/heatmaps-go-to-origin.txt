Every heatmap has a "Go to origin (End2End)" link at the very top left of the page, above the title. Clicking it opens the End2End view the heatmap was built from.

This matters because a heatmap only shows two dimensions of the chain. When a cell raises a question about the third — which customers are behind these supplier and product combinations — going back to the origin lets you see the whole Supplier, Product and Customer chain again.

The reverse route also exists: from the End2End view, the Heatmap button in a link area lists the heatmaps built on that relation, and from a heatmap grid the "Show in End2End" button carries the current records back into the End2End view as a selection.
