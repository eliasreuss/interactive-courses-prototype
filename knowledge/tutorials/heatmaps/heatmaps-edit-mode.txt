Click the gear icon at the far right of the heatmap toolbar to turn on edit mode. The button changes to a red "Edit mode: ON" and the extra controls appear:

A pencil icon next to the heatmap name for renaming it.
A colour swatch with a pencil, to the left of the colour legend, opening the Color settings window.
An Add Filter link and slicer fields for Suppliers, Products and Customers above the toolbar. A data source that is not part of this heatmap is shown greyed out and cannot be used.
Hide total, a decimals button and a percentage button in the toolbar.
Save and Save as at the right.

Everything you change takes effect on screen immediately so you can judge the result, but nothing is stored until you click Save. Click the gear again to leave edit mode.
