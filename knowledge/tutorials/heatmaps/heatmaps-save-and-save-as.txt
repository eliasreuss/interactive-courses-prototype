Changes made in edit mode — dimensions, measure, colour scale, totals, number format, filters and slicers — are visible immediately but are only stored when you save. The two buttons sit at the right of the edit-mode bar:

Save — writes the changes back to the heatmap you have open. Because heatmaps are shared, this changes it for every colleague who can see it.
Save as — leaves the original untouched and creates a new heatmap from your current settings, with the name prefilled as the original plus " - Copy". Rename it and click Create.

Drill-downs and cell selections are not part of what gets saved; they are exploration only.

If you are experimenting on a heatmap that colleagues rely on, use Save as. If you have made changes you do not want to keep, leave the page without saving.
