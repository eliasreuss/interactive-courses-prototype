The dropdown in the middle of the heatmap toolbar selects which column is measured in the cells — for example Cost Amount. The aggregation used is printed in italics right after it, for example "sum", so you can always see whether the cells are totals, averages or counts.

Click the dropdown to pick a different measure. A small icon before each name shows its data type, with # marking numeric columns.

The measure and its aggregation are also listed at the top of the About this Heatmap window, under "This Heatmap shows".

Changing the measure rebuilds the colour scale as well, because the colour bands are derived from the values currently in the matrix. A heatmap that looked mostly green can turn mostly red simply because you switched to a measure with a different distribution — that is the scale adapting, not the business changing.
