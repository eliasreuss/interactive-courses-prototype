In edit mode, the Hide total button in the toolbar removes both the Total row and the Total column from the matrix. The button then reads Show total; click it to bring them back.

Hiding the totals is mostly a readability choice. The totals are usually much larger than the individual cells, and on a proportional colour scale they can dominate the colouring and flatten the differences you actually wanted to see.

Note that the sort arrows live in the Total headers, so with the totals hidden you lose the quickest way to reorder rows and columns. Sort first, then hide.

Click Save to store the choice with the heatmap.
