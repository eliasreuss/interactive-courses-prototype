In edit mode, click the small colour swatch with a pencil, just left of the colour legend, to open the Color settings window. It contains:

Scope — whether the colouring is calculated across the whole matrix (Global), within each Row, or within each Column.
Type — Ranked or Proportional. A sentence under the two dropdowns always spells out what the current combination does, for example "Colors show overall rank across the entire Heatmap."
Presets — six ready-made gradients, including red-to-green in both directions and single-colour scales.
A custom gradient bar with colour stops beneath it. Click a stop to change its colour, or click the "×" under a stop to remove it, and the gradient rebuilds.
Preview — a strip of sample values from 1 to 100 showing how the scale will look before you commit.

Click Apply to use the scale, or Cancel to discard. Then click Save in the toolbar to store it with the heatmap.
