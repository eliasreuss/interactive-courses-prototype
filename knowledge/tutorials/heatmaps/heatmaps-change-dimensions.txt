The two dropdowns on the left of the heatmap toolbar control what the matrix is built from. The first, marked with a down arrow, is the row categorisation and carries the icon of the data source it comes from. The second, marked with a right arrow, is the column categorisation.

Click either dropdown to open a searchable list of the categorisation columns available on that data source — for a supplier axis that might be Country, Payment Terms, Purchaser, Supplier Categorization and Supplier Group. The column currently in use is shown in bold. Click a different one and the matrix rebuilds immediately.

This is the quickest way to interrogate the same measure from several angles: keep Double ABC across the top and switch the rows from Supplier Categorization to Country to see whether a cost pattern is about supplier quality or about geography.

Changes take effect straight away on screen but are only stored when you click Save in edit mode.
