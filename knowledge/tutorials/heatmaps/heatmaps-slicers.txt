In edit mode, three slicer fields appear above the toolbar — Suppliers, Products and Customers — each with a green + button and the text "No slicers".

Click the + button under a data source to open a searchable column picker, click a column, and the Data slicer dialog opens where you choose a condition and the values to include. Click Save in that dialog and the slicer is applied; the matrix and the colour scale recalculate around it.

A data source that is not part of this heatmap is shown greyed out and in italics and cannot be sliced — a supplier-against-product heatmap, for example, has the Customers field disabled.

Remove a slicer by clicking the "×" on its chip. Active slicers are listed in the About this Heatmap window, which is the first place to check when a heatmap shows less data than you expect.

Click Save in the toolbar to store the slicers with the heatmap for everyone.
