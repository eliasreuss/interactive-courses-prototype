The Actions section on the Home page gives you a quick view of your active Action items without needing to navigate to the full Actions module.

Each row in the list shows:
- A number badge — the count of items inside that action.
- The action name.
- The last updated date.
- A small recurring icon, indicating the action has a recurring schedule.

Clicking any action row takes you directly into that action, where you can see the full list of items, mark them as done, and manage them.

The Actions section on the Home page is a read-only summary — you can't create or delete actions from here. To manage actions in full, click Actions in the top menu bar.
