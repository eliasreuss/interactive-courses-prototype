The filter bar runs across the top of the Home page, just below the menu. It contains dropdown filters — by default, Customer Group, Supplier Group, and Country.

To filter the KPI data shown on the page, click any of these dropdowns and select a value. The KPI cards will update instantly to show only data that matches your selection.

To remove a filter, click the same dropdown again and select the blank option at the top of the list.

You can also add a temporary personal filter by clicking the green + button on the left side of the filter bar. This opens a searchable list of available metrics and dimensions — click one to add it as a filter for your current session. These temporary filters are not saved and won't affect what other users see.

Filters only affect what you see while you're on the page. They don't change the underlying data or anyone else's view.
