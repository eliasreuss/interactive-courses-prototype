You can choose which Insights appear in the Insights section at the bottom of the Home page.

1. Scroll to the Insights section at the bottom of the Home page.
2. Click the Edit button next to the Insights heading.
3. A window called Select Insights opens with a searchable list of all available Insights.
4. Click any Insight name in the list to add it. It will appear in the Selected Insights area at the bottom of the window, shown as a tag with an × to remove it.
5. To remove an Insight from the Home page, click the × on its tag in the Selected Insights area.
6. Click Save.

Use the Search field at the top of the window to quickly find an Insight by name.
