The Insights section at the bottom of the Home page is a personalised list of Insights you've chosen to pin there for quick access. It saves you from going through the full Insights library every time you want to open a specific one.

Each row shows the Insight name with a small document icon. Clicking an Insight name opens it directly.

Next to the Insights heading you'll see two buttons:
- + New Insight — starts the process of creating a brand-new Insight, right from the Home page.
- Edit — lets you choose which Insights appear in this section.

The Insights list here is personal to you — choosing which Insights to pin here doesn't affect what other users see on their Home page.
