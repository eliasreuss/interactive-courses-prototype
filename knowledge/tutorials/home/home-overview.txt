The Home page is the first thing you see when you log in to Inact Now. Click Home in the top menu bar at any time to return to it.

It brings together your most important information in one place, organised into three sections stacked on top of each other:

KPI cards fill the top of the page. These show the key metrics from whichever dashboard has been pinned to Home — for example, OTD Outbound % or Inventory Turnover Ratio. Each card shows the current value, whether it's on track against a target, and how it's been trending. Large cards include a chart showing performance over recent quarters.

Actions appear below the KPI cards. This is a list of your active Action items — the tasks and work items being tracked in Inact Now. Each row shows the action name, a count of items inside it, and its last updated date. Clicking any action takes you directly into it.

Insights appear at the bottom. This is a list of Insights you've chosen to pin to your Home page for quick access. Clicking an Insight name opens it.

Along the top of the page is a filter bar with dropdown filters (such as Customer Group, Supplier Group, and Country). Selecting a value here filters the KPI cards to show only data matching that selection.
