The Users tab shows a table of all active users with columns: Name, Email, Job Title, Department, Location, Role, and Last Login.

At the top you'll find a search bar (search by name, email, or job title), a filter dropdown, and a counter showing how many users are displayed. The filter dropdown lets you narrow the list by: Unactivated, Active Users, Subscriptions, or New.

Selecting all users via the header checkbox activates a bulk action bar at the bottom.

At the bottom of the page is a "Disabled users (N)" button that opens a separate list of deactivated accounts.
