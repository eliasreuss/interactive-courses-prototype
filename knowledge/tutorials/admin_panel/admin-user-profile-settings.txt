Each user can edit their own settings via their profile page (accessible from the account menu in the top navigation). The page has three sections:

Edit — Change your First name and Last name, then click Save.
Password — Enter your Old password, a New password, and Confirm new password, then click Confirm password to update.
Email preferences — Three radio button options: Receive no emails, Receive email notifications (with an info tooltip), or Receive a daily digest of unread notifications. Click Save preferences to apply.
