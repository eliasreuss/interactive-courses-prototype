Two-factor authentication is available on the user profile settings page. The status shows as "Disabled" by default.

To enable it: download an authenticator app on your mobile device (Microsoft Authenticator is recommended), then click the Enable 2FA button on the profile page and follow the steps in your authenticator app.

Once enabled, you'll need to enter a one-time passcode every time you log into Inact Now.
