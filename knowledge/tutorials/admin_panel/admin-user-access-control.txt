The Access control tab in the user edit dialog is a two-panel picker. The left panel shows datasources the user does not have access to; the right panel shows datasources they do have access to.

Use the "Select all" button to grant access to everything at once, or click individual items to move them. The "Deselect all" button removes all granted access.

There's also a search field at the top to find specific datasources quickly. Each datasource in the right panel has an × to remove it individually.
