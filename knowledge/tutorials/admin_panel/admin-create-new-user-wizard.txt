Click + New User to open the four-step "Create new user" wizard. The steps are:

Step 1 — Basic info: Email (required), Firstname (required), Lastname (required), Phone number, Language — English or Danish (required), Job Title (required), Department (required), Location (optional).
Step 2 — Permissions: Choose a role (User, Super User, Admin, Subscription Only) and optionally toggle Simple view or Download Data.
Step 3 — Access control: Assign which datasources the user can see. At least one must be selected or a warning appears: "User will not have access to anything."
Step 4 — Homepage dashboards: Optionally add dashboards to the user's home page.

On the final step, you can either click Save (creates the account silently) or Save & Send mail (creates the account and emails the user a welcome invitation).

Validation runs when you click Next — required fields must be filled before you can advance.
