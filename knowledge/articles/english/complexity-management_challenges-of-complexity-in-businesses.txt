Businesses face unprecedented levels of complexity with increasing product introductions, diverse customers and suppliers, demanding customer requirements, and unpredictable market conditions. This complexity leads to higher operating costs, slower response to market opportunities, and a lack of clear understanding of necessary actions for problem-solving.
