index_title: What Are Actions?
title: What Are Actions?
type: main
layout: standard-2-col
---
An Action list is an “alarm” that is triggered when items, customers, or suppliers meet specific criteria. It works as a warning system for your business.

Instead of digging through your data to find problems, the problems come to you. Each morning your list shows exactly what needs attention.
![image/size:550px/align:right/frame:none](now-actions/Action-Table.png)

index_title: Where Do Actions Come From?
title: How Does an Action Work?
type: main
---
Say you want to keep inventory under control. One Action for that is **Dead Stock | Top 10.**

Any item that meets the criteria below lands on the list. Nothing here is fixed. The list reviews your data and updates every day.
![image/size:large/align:center/frame:none](now-actions/Create-Action.png)

Every day, this list surfaces the ten dead stock items costing you the most.

title: How Do I Access an Action?
type: main
---
Getting to your Actions is incredibly simple.

Just like your Insights, your most important Actions are pinned in your Home screen for easy access. To open one, simply click on its name directly from your Home view.
