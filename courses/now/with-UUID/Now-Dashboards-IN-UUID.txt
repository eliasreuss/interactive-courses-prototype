[id:2/topic: Widgets]

slide_id: 4
index_title: Understanding Widgets
title: Understanding Widgets
type: main
layout: standard-2-col
---
Now that you know what Dashboards are, let's explore the individual parts. We will start with Widgets.

Widgets are designed to give you instant answers. 

-**Actual.** Tells you what the actual number (KPI) is. 
-**Target.** The dotted line in the trend and your KPI target. 
-**Latest Development.** How much the KPI moved versus last month, three months ago, or last year.
-**Trend.** Where the KPI landed at the end of each of the last 12 months.

[id:3/topic: Tables]

slide_id: 5
index_title: Understanding Tables
title: Understanding Tables
type: main
---
Right below the widgets you will find tables. A table splits your KPIs by category, for example by Product Group, Company, or Supplier. It can also show a separate list of products, customers, or suppliers.
![image/size:large/align:center/frame:none](learn/images/187/971b4ec51fd5130a2fca7752c86c204bab9bfbbdee7b63c9a9b81b7eaab0a08e)

If you don’t see a table, it may be because your Dashboard only contains widgets, or because you haven’t clicked the small drop-down arrow button.
