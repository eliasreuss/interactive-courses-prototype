[id:2/topic: Where Are We Making Money?]

slide_id: 2
index_title: Where Are We Making Money, and Where Are We Not?
title: Where Are We Making Money, and Where Are We Not?
type: main
layout: standard-2-col
---
A product portfolio can contain thousands of items across many different categories.
Some products sell in high volumes and generate healthy margins. Others sell well but contribute very little profit. Some have attractive margins but never gain enough volume, and some do neither.

The challenge is getting a clear view of where the business is healthy and where it needs attention.

[id:4/topic: Four Categories, Four Different Jobs]

slide_id: 4
index_title: From Performance to Strategy
title: From Performance to Strategy
type: main
---
The KPI Benchmark gives us a detailed view of performance. But with thousands of products, we still need a simple way to decide where to focus.

This is where categorization helps. We look at every product through two commercial lenses: **Revenue** and **Margin**.
![image/size:large/align:center/frame:none](placeholder-images/Axes-empty.png)
