title: Growth and Maturity
type: main
---
We start with our established products. Here, we need to decide where to focus.

With thousands of products in the portfolio, we cannot give them all the same attention. Category Management groups products into Protect, Develop, Grow and Turnaround, so we can focus on the products that matter most and ask the right questions for each category.
![image/size:large/align:center/frame:none](products-intro/PLC-Growth-Maturity.png)

Over time, some products stop earning their place in the portfolio.

But poor performance does not automatically mean a product should leave. Before we phase it out, we also need to understand who is buying it. Linked Revenue helps us make that connection.
