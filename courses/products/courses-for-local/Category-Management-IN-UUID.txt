A product portfolio can contain thousands of items across many different categories.

Some products sell in high volumes and generate healthy margins. Others sell well but contribute very little profit. Some have attractive margins but never gain enough volume, and some do neither.

Looking at the right KPIs helps us get a clear view of where the business is healthy and where it needs attention.

title: The Four Categories
type: main
---
So, what are the categories based on?

We look at each product through two commercial lenses: **Revenue and Margin.**

Similar to the Double ABC Analysis, each product is assessed on both measures. Its combination of revenue and margin determines which category it belongs to.

![image/size:large/align:center/frame:none](products-category-management/Axes-And-Categroies-With-Icons.png)
