Your business depends on introducing new products successfully, but not every product makes it.

The New Product Introduction framework monitors your products during their first 12 months, so you can spot your future cash cows and the products that are falling behind.

The Category Management framework groups your products by performance, so you can dive into your cash cows, run a long-tail analysis and identify the products that erode your profits.
