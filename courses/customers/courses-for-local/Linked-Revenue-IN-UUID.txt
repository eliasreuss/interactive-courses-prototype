[id:2/topic: Introducing Linked Revenue]

slide_id: b8c94b44-70d6-43f9-aebc-5dcd3c56ca9a
index_title: Introducing Linked Revenue
title: Introducing Linked Revenue
type: main
layout: standard-2-col
---
Linked Revenue connects customers with the products they buy.

Instead of asking whether a customer or product is good on its own, we look at the relationship between them.

This helps us understand which relationships support the business and which ones create unnecessary complexity.
![video/size:large/align:right/frame:none](https://pub-3c39fac4c588469d9bf40bf9e56bdbaf.r2.dev/Customers/ABC-Linkage-Animation.mp4)
---

slide_id: 947edff7-c474-4e26-8cb5-39ae200a8868
index_title: One Product Can Tell Two Different Stories
title: One Product Can Tell Two Different Stories
type: main
layout: standard-2-col
---
Looking at a product alone can lead us to the wrong conclusion.

A C item may have low revenue and few order lines. On its own, it can look like an obvious candidate for removal. But what if one of your most important customers depends on it?

Now the relationship changes the decision.
![image/size:large/align:right/frame:none](customers-linked-revenue/Important-Relationship.png)

[id:3/topic: Healthy and Unhealthy Complexity]

slide_id: a19d7b68-8f6f-4d74-9816-07d6c203d5b1
index_title: Healthy Complexity
title: Healthy Complexity
type: main
layout: standard-2-col
---
Some complexity is worth keeping.

An important customer may buy a mix of strong products and products that look weak on their own. Those weaker products still support an important customer relationship.

That makes them part of healthy complexity.

The **C Items Sold to Protect Customers Insight** in Inact Now shows you C items that one or more of your most important customers buys.
![image/size:large/align:right/frame:none](customers-linked-revenue/Healthy-Complexity.png)
---

slide_id: d6423881-a584-4764-a391-92376146d3a9
index_title: Unhealthy Complexity
title: Unhealthy Complexity
type: main
layout: standard-2-col
---
Other relationships create complexity without enough value behind them. A weak customer may depend on products that few or no other customers buy.

Those relationships can add products, inventory and work without contributing enough to the business.
![image/size:large/align:right/frame:none](customers-linked-revenue/Unhealthy-Complexity.png)
