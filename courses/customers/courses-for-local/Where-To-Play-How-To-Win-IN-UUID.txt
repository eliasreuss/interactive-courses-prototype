slide_id: b218a124-23df-4e0b-849b-2203afbfcedb
index_title: Kill It or Bill It
title: Kill It or Bill It
type: main
layout: standard-2-col
---
Let’s make this more concrete.

In the previous course, we found that some C products are bought by important customers. Those products may look weak on their own, but the customer relationship makes them important. We can therefore choose to treat them as strategic.

Once those relationships are protected, we can look at the remaining C products. One possible policy is **Kill it or Bill it.**

Price the product so it earns the profit you require. If customers will not buy it at that price, phase it out. The goal is not to remove every C product. It is to make a deliberate decision about what stays, what goes and why.
![image/size:550px/align:right/frame:none](customers-where-to-play-how-to-win/Kill-Or-Bill.png)
