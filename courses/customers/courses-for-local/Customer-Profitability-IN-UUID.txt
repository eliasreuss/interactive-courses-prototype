[id:3/topic: Profitability Is Only Part of the Picture]

slide_id: def8a787-4753-4b9a-9756-0c3d72e0cd2b
index_title: Profitability Is Only Part of the Picture
title: Profitability Is Only Part of the Picture
type: main
layout: standard-2-col
---
Knowing whether a customer is profitable is important.

But profitability alone does not tell us what it takes to serve that customer.

A customer may depend on a wide range of products, inventory and suppliers. Some of those relationships may be critical to the business, while others may create unnecessary complexity.

To understand the full picture, we need to connect the customer to the rest of the value chain. This is exactly what we will explore in the next courses
