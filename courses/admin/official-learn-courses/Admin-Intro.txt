index_title: Not Everyone Has the Same Access
title: Not Everyone Has the Same Access
type: main
---
In Inact Now, your role determines what you can see and what you can do in the platform.

There are four different user types, each with its own level of access and capabilities. Some roles focus on exploring and using existing content, while others can create, edit, and share across the organization.

Understanding your role will help you know what’s available to you and what to expect as you navigate the platform.
![image/size:large/align:center/frame:none](learn/images/190/0667a8a2f76ce3d14f02059c6b28100de09d393159d3d8ed9513e15168212615)

index_title: What Is Your Role?
title: What Is Your Role?
type: main
layout: standard-2-col
---
The quickest way to find out what your role is, is to look at your user color in the top navigation bar in Inact Now. Green means you are a User. Blue means you are a Super User. Purple means you are an Admin.

If you only see a Home tab, you are a Home Only User.
![image/size:400px/align:right/frame:none](learn/images/191/e2b28d177b59000ac171028ef77bb2422e8da891d6f0c143b47a124d6527fed4)
